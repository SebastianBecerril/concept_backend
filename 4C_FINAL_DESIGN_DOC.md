# Assignment 4C: Final Design Document

Overall, I would say that over the past couple months my original idea that I cam up with during the problem framing assignment has changed so drastically. From the purpose, to the what I though the layout would look like, to the features, it all changed as I went through my own iteration and design process. As such, I will be laying out the primary changes that my project *StudyCircle* underwent throughout the different assignment stages. 

## Assignment 2: Functional Design 

Since this assignment, my project has changed in almost every single aspect, which I will specify:

1. **Problem Statement and Functionality**
	1. This is the part of my project that has stayed mostly constant throughout the whole time. The purpose and application pitch still can be identified through the app functionality and it works decently well according to what was initially specified 
2. **Concept Design**
	1. This is where the drastic changes begin to appear. Initially, I had just thought up of four concepts: CourseCatalog, UserEnrollments, CourseSpace, and CommunityBoard.
	2. When I started my backend implementation, I decided to scrap CourseSpace, as it added unnecessary complexity to the app that was not necessarily innovative and it just would have taken extra time to implement that could have been better spent (and it was) enhancing the other features. This was the only concept I completely scrapped.
	3. When I was making my initial concept design, i definelty overlooked many major aspects of my application, with the main two being user management and how communities would be implemented. As such, I decided to add three more concepts: Community which would manage the community aspect, UserAuthentication which managed authentication and privacy, and UserProfiles which just allowed users to have an identity in my application. 
	4. One final point to make was that initially, I thought that CourseCatalog would use a similar style to MIT"s hydrant, where users can just elect courses. However, I discovered that this was a lot more complicated than initially expected, so I ended up going for a user curated course catalog. This, while not as optimal or streamlined, allows for good functionality that still serves the purpose of the app without being overly complex. 
3. **User Interface Sketches**
	1. For this, my main comment is that while the essence of my sketches was definitely driving me while making my front end.  They did not exactly translate to the final product. This was mainly due to the fact that I unfortunately did not completely think about the whole UI/UX at this stage, so I made some very simplistic sketches. 

## Assignment 4:

In assignment 4, my design changes were not so much premeditated as they were me just adapting to the errors and gaps in design that I would run into while coding. For example, the use of Context allowed me to very quickly and thoroughly flesh out my initial concept ideas in a much better way than I could have done by myself.  

Overall, my experience in assignment 4 in my design iteration did not change much from the end of assignment 4a to the end of assignment 4c. As I was implementing my front end, I discovered some design flaws, such as lack of some queries (like getting all memberships at once), or implementing some actions that came in handy. However, most of my focus in this part was just making my website to the aesthetic image that I had in my head. It was very enjoyable iterating with the LLM and creating a UI/UX that aligned with the purpose of my application.
